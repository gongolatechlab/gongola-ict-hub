FVG (Fair Value Gap)

An imbalance in the price where one candle's wick leaves a gap between the previous candle's high and the next candle's low. Price often returns to fill this gap.

🧠 +FVG (Blue): Bullish gap. -FVG (Red): Bearish gap.

Order Block (OB)

The last opposing candle before a strong move that breaks market structure. This is where institutions have placed their orders, and price often reacts here.

DOL (Draw on Liquidity)

A specific price level (like a previous high/low or equal highs/lows) where a large amount of liquidity (stop losses) is resting. The market is drawn to these levels.

PDH / PDL

Previous Day High and Previous Day Low. These are key reference points used by day traders to gauge daily bias and potential reversal zones.

Killzones (London / NY)

Specific time windows when institutional trading volume is highest.
London Killzone: 2:00 AM - 5:00 AM NY Time.
New York Killzone: 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM NY Time.

iFVG (Inversed FVG)

When a Fair Value Gap fails and price breaks through it, the FVG becomes invalidated and flips its role. A failed +FVG becomes a bearish -iFVG, and a failed -FVG becomes a bullish +iFVG.
