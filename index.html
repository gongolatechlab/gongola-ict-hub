Research & Intelligence

Understand the market. Study the structure.

We do not trade on tips or indicators alone. We study the underlying mechanics of price delivery. Here are the core concepts that form our analytical framework.

Liquidity Engineering

How institutions accumulate and distribute positions by engineering liquidity pools at key highs and lows.

BSL & SSL

Buy-side and Sell-side liquidity. The resting orders above old highs and below old lows that act as magnets for price.

Market Structure Shift (MSS)

The first sign of a trend reversal. Occurs when price breaks a key swing point with momentum after a liquidity sweep.

Fair Value Gaps (FVG)

Inefficiencies left behind by rapid price movement. These gaps often act as support or resistance when retested.

Order Blocks

The last candle before a strong move. These zones are where institutions may have accumulated positions.

Turtle Soup

A false breakout strategy that traps retail traders. We look for the sweep and reversal.
